# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

### 1.1 Product Name

**University Class Scheduling and Room Allocation System with Conflict Detection**

### 1.2 Purpose

The system is designed to assist academic schedulers in generating, managing, and optimizing class schedules for a specific department. It automates scheduling while allowing manual adjustments with built-in conflict detection.

### 1.3 Scope

* Applies to **one department only**
* Generates schedules **per semester**
* Supports **lecture and laboratory classes**
* Focuses on **faculty load-based scheduling**

---

## 2. Goals & Objectives

### 2.1 Goals

* Automate schedule generation based on professor load
* Minimize scheduling conflicts
* Provide flexibility for manual edits
* Ensure fair distribution of teaching loads
* Improve visibility of room and professor availability

### 2.2 Success Metrics

* Reduced scheduling time
* Zero unresolved conflicts in final schedule
* Balanced professor workload within limits
* Accurate room allocation based on type

---

## 3. Users & Roles

### 3.1 Super Admin

* Manage professors, courses, rooms
* Maintain system data

### 3.2 Scheduler/Admin

* Generate schedules
* Edit schedules manually
* Assign professor loads
* Manage academic ranking constraints

### 3.3 Viewer (Instructor)

* View assigned schedule only

---

## 4. Core Features

## 4.1 Schedule Generation

* Generates schedules per semester
* Uses **professor load (professor_load table)**
* Assigns:

  * Section
  * Course
  * Professor
  * Room
  * Day and Time

### Rules:

* Must match **exact subject assigned to professor**
* Must respect:

  * Academic ranking limits (min/max hours & units)
  * Course lecture/lab hours
  * Number of sections per subject
* If unavailable:

  * Assign **TBA (To Be Announced)**

---

## 4.2 Conflict Detection

* Detects:

  * Professor conflicts (overlapping schedules)
  * Room conflicts
  * Time conflicts
* Triggered:

  * During generation
  * During manual edits

---

## 4.3 Professor Load Management

* Assign courses to professors
* Specify **number of sections per subject**
* Real-time calculation:

  * Total hours = (lecture + lab hours) × sections
  * Total units = units × sections

### Constraints:

* Based on **Academic Ranking**
* Cannot exceed:

  * Max hours
  * Max units

---

## 4.4 Academic Ranking System

* New table: `academic_ranking`
* Linked to program (locked per scheduler)

### Fields:

* min_hours
* max_hours
* min_units
* max_units

### Behavior:

* Replaces professor-level limits
* Controls scheduling eligibility

---

## 4.5 Section Handling

* Sections auto-generated:

  * Format: `1A`, `1B`, etc.
* Based on:

  * Number of sections input

### Rules:

* 1st Year:

  * Max 2 courses/day (once per week)
* 2nd Year:

  * Max 2 late classes (>5PM) per week
* 3rd/4th Year:

  * Prefer 2 courses/day

---

## 4.6 Room Allocation

* Room types:

  * Lecture rooms
  * Laboratory rooms

### Rules:

* Lecture → Lecture rooms only
* Lab → Lab rooms only
* If unavailable → TBA

---

## 4.7 Working Hours

* Schedule range: **7:00 AM – 8:00 PM**

### Working Hours Fields:

* start_day
* end_day
* start_time
* lunch_time
* end_time

### Rules:

* No classes during lunch_time
* Lunch not shown in room availability

---

## 4.8 Schedule Management

### Active vs Archived:

* New schedule → `archive = false`
* Old schedules → `archive = true`

### Rules:

* Only **one active schedule per semester**
* Prevent generation if same semester already active

---

## 4.9 Manual Editing

* Drag/drop or edit schedule entries
* Conflict detection runs immediately
* No modal interruption when editing cards

---

## 4.10 Professor Dashboard

* Displays:

  * Total assigned hours
  * Total units
  * Remaining capacity

---

## 4.11 Backup & Restore

* Backup current system data
* Restore via upload
* Supabase-compatible

---

## 6. System Constraints

* Must use **professor_load (NOT prof_course)**
* Schedule must reference:

  * `prof_load_id` only
* Remove:

  * direct professor_id / course_id in schedule
